</div>Everything you need to stay on track
📷 Scan receipts in seconds
Take a photo or choose a receipt from your gallery. AI helps identify the merchant, date, total, and category, so you can review and save the expense with less effort.
✍️ Add expenses your way
No receipt? No problem. Record a purchase manually, whether it's a cash payment, a quick snack, or something you bought online.
📈 See the bigger picture
Get a clear overview of daily and monthly expenses, recent activity, and how your spending compares with your budget.
🧾 Keep your history organized
Find past purchases and update details whenever you need to. Your expense history helps you recognize patterns over time.
🎯 Set a budget that works for you
Choose a monthly spending goal and follow your progress in a visual, easy-to-understand way.
🔔 Stay informed, not overwhelmed
Opt in to gentle expense reminders and budget alerts that help you stay mindful of your spending.
🔐 Your information matters
SnapSpend is designed around private user accounts and thoughtful handling of spending data. AI suggestions can be reviewed and corrected before they are saved.
